
\begin{abstract}
    \textbf{Collective deterrence for a coalition of states depends on two key principles: the institutional design of decision-making, formalized as a social choice function that aggregates states individual levels of resolve, and the coalitions available escalation technology. In this paper we study the interactions of these principles via a multi actor signalling game, with a focus on understanding and eliminating weak-link equilibria. This model provides a simple formal framework for understanding how small alliances, such as the Nordic countries, can maintain credible deterrence without external guarantees.} 
\end{abstract}
