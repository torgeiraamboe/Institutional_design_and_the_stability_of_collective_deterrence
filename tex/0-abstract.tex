
\begin{abstract}
    \textbf{Collective deterrence models for a coalition of
states depends on two key principles: the insti-
tutional design of decision-making, formalized
as a social choice function that aggregates states
individual levels of resolve, and the coalitions
available escalation technology. In this paper
we study the interactions of these principles
via a multi actor signalling game, with a fo-
cus on understanding and eliminating weak-link
equilibria. This model provides a simple for-
mal framework for understanding how small al-
liances, such as the Nordic countries, can main-
tain credible deterrence without external guar-
antees.} 
\end{abstract}
