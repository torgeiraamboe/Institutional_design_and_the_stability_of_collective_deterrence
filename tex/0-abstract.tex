
\begin{abstract}
    \textbf{Deterrence coalitions that collectively own their deterrence technology need an institutional design to decide when to retaliate against an attack or incident. This choice of institutional design, formalized through a social choice function, introduces a tradeoff between credible deterrence and escalation control. We study this tradeoff via a simple signalling model, and the associated binary classification problem. We compute the perfect Bayesian equilibria, and study the optimal institutional designs for both large and small coalitions by computing a variety of binormal and empirical ROC curves.} 
\end{abstract}
